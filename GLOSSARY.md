# Quiz App

A live trivia game: a Host runs a Quiz in real time and Players answer on their own devices.

## Language

### Content

**Quiz**:
Reusable trivia content owned by a Host: a Topic, a Difficulty, and a fixed, ordered list of Questions. Generated by AI and reviewed by its Host before it can be played.
_Avoid_: Question set, quiz set, deck, round

**Draft Quiz**:
A newly generated Quiz its Host is still reviewing; Questions may be discarded or regenerated, and it cannot be played.
_Avoid_: Pending quiz, unpublished quiz

**Ready Quiz**:
A Quiz its Host has approved. It can be played and can never be changed; a different Quiz must be generated instead.
_Avoid_: Published quiz, final quiz, live quiz

**Topic**:
The free-text subject a Quiz is about, e.g. "90s Britpop".
_Avoid_: Category, theme, subject

**Difficulty**:
How hard a Quiz's Questions are: Easy, Medium or Hard.
_Avoid_: Level

**Time Limit**:
How long Players have to answer a Question. Each Quiz has a default, which an individual Question may override.
_Avoid_: Timer, duration, countdown

**Question**:
One multiple-choice prompt within a Quiz, with 2–4 Options of which exactly one is correct.
_Avoid_: Item, card

**Option**:
One of the possible answers to a Question.
_Avoid_: Choice, answer (reserve "answer" for what a Player submits)

### Live play

**Game**:
One live run of a Quiz, with its own Players, answers and scores. A Quiz can be played in many Games.
_Avoid_: Session, room, match, lobby

**Lobby**:
The phase of a Game before its first Question, and the only time Players may join.
_Avoid_: Waiting room, pre-game

**Question Open**:
The phase of a Game in which the current Question is shown and Answers are accepted, until its Time Limit expires or every Player has answered.
_Avoid_: Active question, live question

**Reveal**:
The phase of a Game after a Question closes, when the correct Option and updated Scores are shown.
_Avoid_: Results, answer screen, intermission

**Finished**:
The final phase of a Game, reached after the last Reveal or when the Game is abandoned; its final Leaderboard is kept in the Host's history.
_Avoid_: Ended, complete, over, closed

**Join Code**:
A short code shown by the Host that Players enter to join a specific Game during its Lobby.
_Avoid_: PIN, room code, game ID

**Answer**:
The single Option a Player submits for a Question; it cannot be changed once submitted.
_Avoid_: Response, guess, vote

**Score**:
Points a Player earns in a Game. A correct Answer earns 500–1000 points, more the faster it was given; a wrong or missing Answer earns 0.
_Avoid_: Points total, rating

**Leaderboard**:
The Players of a Game ranked by Score; Players with equal Scores share a rank. A Finished Game's final Leaderboard is kept in the Host's history.
_Avoid_: Scoreboard, standings, rankings

### People

**Host**:
A person with an account who owns Quizzes and runs Games of them.
_Avoid_: Author, admin, quizmaster, owner

**Player**:
An anonymous participant in a single Game, identified only by their Nickname.
_Avoid_: Taker, contestant, user, guest

**Nickname**:
The name a Player chooses when joining a Game. It must be unique within that Game and must pass a toxicity check.
_Avoid_: Username, handle, display name, alias
