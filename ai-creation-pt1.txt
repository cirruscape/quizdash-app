BUILDING A QUIZ APP WITH CLAUDE CODE: PART 1
============================================

This is the input and output of a single Claude Code session, which took an
empty directory to a designed and scaffolded application.

  Tool:     Claude Code (CLI), model Claude Opus 5.5
  Dates:    2026-10-24 to 2026-10-25
  Starting point: an empty directory, /home/bryce/dev/quizapp

How to read this:
  USER    is what the developer typed, verbatim.
  CLAUDE  is Claude's reply as shown in the terminal. The question rounds are
          reproduced in full. Long final summaries are shortened slightly.
  [ ... ] marks an action Claude took with its tools (reading or writing files,
          running commands, searching the web). These summarise the action;
          they are not the raw tool output.

plugin. It interviews the developer in rounds of questions, and records agreed
terms in a GLOSSARY.md and agreed decisions as ADRs (Architecture Decision
Records) as it goes.

These are the root questions. Everything else depends on them.

Q1 - What is a quiz app *for*? Who uses it and why?
  (a) Self-study / spaced repetition: one person drills their own question
      sets, like Anki.
  (b) Classroom / assessment: someone authors a Quiz, others take it, and
      results are scored and reviewed.
  (c) Live party/trivia: a host runs a game in real time and players answer
      on their phones, like Kahoot.
  (d) Something else.
  -> I'd guess (b), author-and-take.

Q2 - Who are the actors? Under (b): the Author creates Quizzes and the Taker
     answers them. Can one person be both? Are Takers anonymous, or do they
     have identities whose history persists?
  -> One person can be both. Takers must be identified so their results
     persist.

Q3 - Scope of this first version: is this a learning or side project, or
     something meant to ship to real users?
  -> It's a small personal project, so we optimise for simplicity.

Q4 - What does "quiz" mean to you? Is a Quiz a fixed, ordered list of
     Questions, or a pool that each attempt draws from?
  -> It's a fixed, ordered list of Questions for v1.

CLAUDE:
Live trivia changes the shape a lot. Your "yes" to Q2 was answering a question

Q1 - Roles: Author, Host, Player. Should the Author and Host be the same
     person, with an account that owns their Quizzes? Should Players be
     anonymous, joining with a nickname and keeping no history afterwards?
  -> Yes. The Host has an account and owns their Quizzes. Players are
     anonymous and only exist for one Game.

Q2 - Quiz vs Game. A Quiz is reusable content: the questions and answers. A
     Game is one live run of a Quiz, with its Players, answers and scores.
  -> Use Quiz and Game as defined.

Q3 - Quiz structure: a fixed, ordered list of Questions?
  -> Yes for v1.

Q4 - Joining: a short Join Code, say 6 characters, shown by the Host, plus a
     nickname. No login.
  -> Yes. Nicknames must be unique within a Game.

Q5 - Where Questions are displayed:
  (a) Kahoot style: the Host's screen shows the Question, and phones show
      only answer buttons.
  (b) Self-contained: every device shows the full Question.
  -> (b). It's simpler and works remotely.

Q6 - Pacing: the Host advances, and each Question has a timer.
Q7 - Question types: multiple choice only, with 2-4 options and one correct.
Q8 - Scoring: speed-weighted, with 500-1000 points for a correct answer.
